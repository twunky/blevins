# Candidate CTX-1.23 — Phase 7.5 End-to-End UX & User-Test Readiness

The project owner approved this roadmap-priority update on 2026-09-27 and authorized context-only preparation, verification, and activation if verification passes. `DEC-062` records the decision. `CTX-1.23` is active after passing `VER-CTX-1.23`; `CTX-1.22` remains an approved historical baseline.

## Approved roadmap effect

- Add **Phase 7.5 — End-to-End UX & User-Test Readiness** after Phase 7 and before Phase 8 in implementation priority. Phase 7.5 is `NEXT / PLANNED`; it may begin only after Phase 7 is formally complete.
- Keep Phase 7 `CURRENT` until its own approved completion and verification criteria are satisfied. The Phase 7 status and WI-007-005 authorization state are not changed by this decision.
- The Phase 7.5 objective is: “A new or returning user can enter the application, establish their approved Family context, understand their role, navigate the implemented product areas, perform ordinary family coordination workflows, and return to a useful Home experience without developer guidance.”
- Phase 7.5 focuses on first-use/return-user journeys, invitation-based Family entry, the app shell/navigation, useful attention-first Home and empty states, understandable structural Family administration, integration of implemented Family/Event/Plan/Trip workflows, and an end-to-end user-test scenario.
- Preserve controlled/operator provisioning of the first Family Group and first Family Administrator under `REQ-FAM-006`; public/self-service Family creation is not introduced. The initial user-test journey begins with an already provisioned Family and an invitation. Later self-service creation requires a separate product/privacy decision and canonical update.
- Phase 7.5 may define user-visible entry/onboarding and distinguish the current Development identity from future real-user sign-in/sign-up expectations. It selects no identity provider, authentication platform, or production account-registration architecture.
- Family Administrator status remains structural and does not grant private profile, Event, Trip, Sharing Session, location, Safety Circle, or unrelated private-data access.
- Home preserves attention-first, family-first, no-surveillance-first, contextual-sharing, and progressive-disclosure principles. Empty states provide concise purpose and authorized next steps without inventing unfinished capabilities.
- `OD-001` remains open; adding Phase 7.5 does not decide the final navigation structure.
- UX mockups remain non-authoritative visual/workflow references; they do not establish product behavior, permission, field, navigation, or workflow-state requirements.
- Exit criteria include coherent first-use/return-user journeys; understandable invitation/join, navigation, and administration; useful Home in empty and populated states; connected implemented Family/Event/Plan/Trip areas; useful empty states; a passing end-to-end test scenario completable without developer explanation; and preserved privacy/audience/sharing boundaries.
- Phase 8 — Safety Coordination retains its existing scope and remains later. Phase 7.5 may prepare shell/Home for future approved Safety information but adds no Safety capabilities.
- Phase 9 — Integration & UX Refinement remains planned for deeper integration, user-feedback-informed refinement, accessibility refinement, performance, broader usability, and release-quality polish.
- Phases 8–11 retain their numbering. Phase 10 Testing & Verification and Phase 11 Pilot & Release Readiness remain unchanged.
- No product requirement IDs are added or changed. Existing UX, Family, privacy, Event, Plan, Trip, and Sharing requirements remain authoritative. New product behavior discovered in planning follows the normal proposal/decision/context-update/story process.
- This decision neither closes Phase 7 nor starts Phase 7.5 implementation, creates implementation stories, modifies application code, or authorizes Safety/authentication/Family-provisioning changes.

## Compatibility and authorization boundary

This is a roadmap and project-priority context update only. Product requirements and application behavior remain unchanged. Phase 7 stays current; Phase 7.5 is next/planned after formal Phase 7 closure. No application tests or implementation changes are authorized by this baseline update.
