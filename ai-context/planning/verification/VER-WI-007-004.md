## Scope and evidence

Trip Notes were added to existing Trip detail, with authenticated Trip-bound list/add/own-edit/own-soft-delete/creator-hide operations. The new `FamilyTripNotes` SQL table is additive. `AUTHOR_DELETED` and `CREATOR_HIDDEN` rows remain in SQL and are excluded from normal responses. Trip audience, Plan, Event, Check-In, Sharing Session, and completion behavior were not changed.
