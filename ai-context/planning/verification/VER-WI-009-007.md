## Behavior

- **`/family` (dashboard):**
  - Header: "Family", the Family name in the subtitle, the date, and the gear.
  - Members card: filter and clear, initials tiles, and the selection state.
  - Member detail: the shared fields only, plus accepted relationships that include the member.
  - 2 × 2 grid: Waiting for you and the three placeholders.
- **`/family/settings`:**
  - Back to Family.
  - Family details (read-only), with the existing membership and administrator privacy statements and a link to `/me`.
  - Invite an adult family member, for administrators only.
  - Family relationships:
    - suggest a relationship;
    - grouped lists: Waiting for you (accept or decline), Sent by you (withdraw), and Accepted relationships (remove with confirmation, for the two adults only).
- **Administrator and member difference:** only the Invite card (send or resend, and revoke), shown when `canAdministerCurrentFamily()` (`/api/family-groups/administered`). Every action is still authorized by the server.
- **Existing functionality preserved:** directory filter, member detail, every relationship action, and invitation administration. Nothing was removed; management moved to Settings.
- **Privacy and authorization unchanged:**
  - The directory shows display names only, never administrator assignments (`DEC-033` / `PRP-008`).
  - Pending suggestions remain visible only to the two adults (`REQ-PRIV-015`).
  - There are no new requests.
- **Navigation:** Family is current with `aria-current="page"` on `/family` and `"true"` on `/family/settings`. The six destinations are unchanged.
