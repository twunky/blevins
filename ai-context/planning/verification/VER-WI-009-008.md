## Behavior

- **Dashboard (`/events`):** a 2 × 2 grid of Upcoming Events, Trips, RSVPs Needed, and Quick Links. DOM (and narrow) order: Upcoming Events, Trips, Quick Links, RSVPs Needed. Without a selected Family there is no subtitle and no Create action; the existing loading and no-Family states remain.
- **Upcoming Events:** `dashboardEvents()` uses the same selection as Home through `upcomingEvents()`: the current Family only, today or later, nearest first (date, time, then ID), capped at 5. Rows have a date tile, a linked name to `/events/:eventId`, date · time, and location. No pill. The empty state is compact and points to All events.
- **Trips:** the existing `/api/trips` read (now also loaded on the Events dashboard) is filtered to the selected Family. A retained creator Trip from another Family, or any Trip from a non-selected Family, never renders.
- **RSVPs Needed:** a heading and one sentence. No button, link, input, image, list, digit, or request.
- **Quick Links:** six semantic links in a `nav` labelled "Quick Links".
- **`/events/new`:** h1 "Create event", Back to Events, and the unchanged form. It makes no item read and no Trips read.
- **`/events/all`:** h1 "All events", Back to Events, and the unchanged list with inline edit. It makes no item read.
- **`/events/:eventId`:** unchanged authorize-first item page; its title is now "Events".
- **Post-create:** navigates to `/events/{eventId}` with the focus-heading state; the item page shows "Event created and shared with the selected family members." only for that navigation, not on refresh or back/forward. A failed create keeps the existing error on `/events/new`.
- **Routes:** `events/new` and `events/all` are declared before `events/:eventId`. Navigation: `/events` has `aria-current="page"`; `/events/new`, `/events/all`, and `/events/:eventId` have `"true"`.
