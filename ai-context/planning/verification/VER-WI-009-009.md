## Implementation

- **Standalone component:** `src/app/safety-dashboard.ts`, `.html`, `.scss`, rendered from `app.html` in `@defer (on immediate)`. It builds as its own lazy `safety-dashboard` chunk (11.25 kB raw, 2.96 kB estimated transfer). No `App` refactor.
- **Concern summary:** `GET /api/concerns` (unchanged), Open only, newest activity first, at most three rows. Row: initials, "Concern about {subject}" linking to `/concerns/:id`, the Open text pill, "Created by you" / "You are a selected participant", the Family name for multi-Family callers, last activity. View all goes to `/concerns`. Empty: "No open Concerns are shared with you."
- **Incident summary:** `GET /api/incidents` (unchanged summaries), Open only, same ordering and limit. Row: initials, "Incident about {subject}" linking to `/incidents/:id`, Open, the coordinator line, the Family name when needed, last activity. View all goes to `/incidents`. Empty: "No open Safety Incidents are shared with you." Footnote: "Contact attempts, tasks, and timelines remain inside each Incident."
- **Omitted panel ④ features (inappropriate, not missing):** Recent Contact Attempts, the combined Safety Timeline and its Today filter, "Monitoring", Contact / Call / Send message, Mark as Resolved on the dashboard, Safety Settings, expected check-in time, last known location, alarm-red surfaces; the redesign board's Emergency options.
- **Dead code removed:** the `safety` branch of `group-landing.ts` was unreachable (its only caller passes `group="plansTrips"`), so it was removed. The Plans & Trips landing is unchanged.
