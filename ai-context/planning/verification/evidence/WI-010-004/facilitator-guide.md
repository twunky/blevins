## One session script

### Opening — read aloud

“We are testing the application, not you. There are no wrong answers. Everything here is made-up practice information; please don't enter your own family information, contacts or location. Some production capabilities are intentionally unavailable in this local test. Please say what you are looking for, what you expect and what seems unclear. I'll give you short situations and may ask follow-up questions. Try them as you normally would; you can pause or stop at any time.”

“You are using a practice adult who belongs to the selected practice Family. The labeled Development identity controls are local test setup, not real sign-in. I'll handle those controls if needed. Any location used today will be synthetic.”

Do not explain architecture, navigation paths, recipient semantics or the Safety Circle/Trusted Contact distinction before discovery.

### During each task

Read only the scenario below. Allow an independent attempt and think-aloud; do not point at navigation. A neutral question is “What are you looking for?” or “What do you expect to happen?” Afterwards ask the task's brief follow-up. Record first interpretation before any clarification. Prior task exposure is part of this ordered session: record if T4 revealed the preset area before T5.

Recovery only when blocked: MINOR = one broad orientation hint without the control/path (e.g. “Where might family organization live?”). MAJOR = naming the area/control, giving the route, repeated directional hints or taking over. Record the exact hint, reason and point where given. Outcome reflects all coaching used, not just the eventual completion. If a defect blocks progress, record BLOCKED BY DEFECT and the visible symptom; do not diagnose beyond evidence or repair during the session. Facilitator test-identity/location setup help is recorded separately from product coaching. Return Home between tasks only after observing the adult's own return attempt; facilitator reset is assistance if that return was part of the goal.

### Closing — 5 minutes

1. What was easiest?
2. What was hardest?
3. Was anything named differently than expected?
4. Was there anywhere you were unsure who could see information?
5. Was there anything you thought the app would do that it did not?
6. Which area would you be least confident using without help?
7. What one change would make the app easier to use?

Thank the adult. Summarize only observed behavior, anonymizing comments; avoid long transcripts or routine screenshots. Ask them not to supply private examples. If a comment contains personal information, record only its non-identifying usability meaning.

## Interpreting the later human evidence

Potential blocking signal: a critical task needs major coaching or cannot be completed; repeated navigation dead end; a privacy misconception could cause unintended sharing; an actual defect prevents completion. A single serious privacy issue can warrant review; do not wait for repetition. Record selected Family versus authorization, circle versus actual recipient access, manual contact versus account access, Check-In visibility, Sharing audience, current point versus history and Concern/Incident access even when behavior is technically correct.

Potential bounded follow-up: repeated confusing terminology, poor feature discovery, misleading copy/empty state or unnecessary backtracking. An acceptable limitation is intentionally deferred behavior clearly communicated, or a production dependency that does not block local understanding. Record an expectation even when it maps to DEC-092; do not implement it. Use participant/task evidence and coaching severity, not a rigid numeric pass rate from 3–5 adults. Actual defects or misunderstandings may warrant an affected matrix row changing to BLOCKED BY DEFECT / NEEDS CLARIFICATION after evidence review; preparation alone changes no journey classification.
