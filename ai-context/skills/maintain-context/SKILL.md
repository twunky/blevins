---
name: maintain-context
description: Apply accepted decisions to canonical context, maintain traceability and status, and prevent duplicate, stale, or unsupported project instructions.
---

1. Read `../../README.md`, `../../project-status.md`, and the accepted decision authorizing the change.
2. Load only the affected canonical documents and requirement-index entries.
3. When bootstrapping, treat `../../bootstrap/product-ux-source.md` as extraction evidence only; flag ambiguity instead of resolving it.
4. Update canonical wording, IDs, links, supersession references, baseline state, and project status together.
5. Keep normative wording in one canonical location and remove no history without an approved supersession path.
6. Verify the context change separately. Do not approve a baseline when sources, conflicts, or verification are incomplete.
