---
name: verify-story
description: Verify a story or phase against canonical requirements and acceptance criteria, with explicit evidence, independence, and pass or blocker reporting.
---

1. Read `../../README.md`, `../../project-status.md`, the subject story or phase, and `../../templates/verification.md`.
2. Load only the canonical requirement sources listed by the subject's `required_context`.
3. Declare the verification `INDEPENDENT` or `SELF-VERIFIED` before evaluating results.
4. Map every cited requirement and acceptance criterion to evidence and `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, or justified `NOT APPLICABLE`.
5. Do not reinterpret requirements, excuse missing evidence, or infer intended behavior.
6. Report limitations and follow-up. Mark a story `DONE` only when its completion rules are satisfied.
